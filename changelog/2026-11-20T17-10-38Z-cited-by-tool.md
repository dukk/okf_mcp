## Added
- `cited_by` MCP tool (issue #761): read-only. Give `source_id` (matched exactly, ignoring case) and/or `resource` (matched exactly, case-sensitive); with both, one `sources` entry must match both. It searches the connection's catalogs (or `catalogs`) and, with `changeset`, that changeset's drafts. Each hit has `catalog`, `catalog_label`, `path`, `title` and the matching `sources` entries, ordered by catalog id then path. At most `limit` hits are returned (default and maximum 200) and `truncated` says more exist. A catalog whose changeset does not apply is listed in `skipped`, as `search` does.
- `okf::search::cites`, `SourceMatch` and `CitedEntry`: the matching, independent of the tool's arguments, so an exact `sources` filter on `search` can call `cites` and carry `Vec<CitedEntry>` on its hits without a rewrite.

## Changed
- The `add_source` and `remove_source` descriptions point at `cited_by`. `docs/reference/tools.md` is regenerated.
- The tool count in the annotation and reference tests goes from 44 to 45, and in the investigator client and maintenance tests from 43 to 44.
- The MCPJam scenario "search by source id" (`test.fails`) is now a plain test that uses `cited_by`.

## Notes
- `title` and `author` are not matched. Ids are unique only within a concept, so use `resource` to find one source across concepts.
