# M17 Task 5: REST labels, `GET /labels` and the edit routes

## Added
- `label` on `UserView`, `TeamView`, `MemberView`, `CatalogSummary` and `AccountView`; `display_name`, `owner_team_label` on `CatalogSummary`; `owned_team_details` (`name`, `display_name`, `label`, `personal`) on `AccountView`, beside the unchanged `owned_teams`; `user_label` on `WhoAmI`. `TeamView::of(record, &Labels)` replaces `From<TeamRecord>`, since a personal team's label needs its user's.
- `GET /api/v1/labels` (`src/http/api/labels.rs`). An every-catalog admin sees every user, team and catalog (catalogs told apart by `Labels::catalog_choices`). Anyone else sees its own user, its teams, the loaded catalogs it reaches and those catalogs' owner teams. A provisioner is refused by the allow-list.
- `PATCH /me` (a console session; 404 without a user, 403 for a key), `PATCH /users/{name}` (managers), `PATCH /teams/{name}` (a manager, or an owner session checked by the store under its locks), and `PATCH /catalogs/{catalog}` with `{display_name?, description?}`: allowed for a provisioner, a catalog admin, or an owner session, which is refused while the team is suspended.
- The three PATCHes join `PROVISIONER_ROUTES`.
- `Labels::users`, `teams` and `catalog_slugs`; `teams::owned` and `teams::OwnedTeam` (replacing `owned_names`).
- Route tests in `src/http/api/labels_tests.rs` and `provisioner_tests.rs`.

## Changed
- `PATCH /catalogs/{catalog}`'s answer keeps `name`, `previous` (the old description, a string) and `description`, and adds `previous_display_name`, `display_name` and `label`. A body with neither field is a 422 (`limit: "body"`).
- `POST /catalogs` answers with the new fields, built from the created row.

## Notes
- The suspended-owner refusal discloses the suspension only to a session that reaches the catalog or is in its owner team; any other session gets the store's `Forbidden`.
- The store layer does not check suspension, so the handler does (epic decision).
- The plan's Task 5 carries a dated correction note for the differences from its text.
