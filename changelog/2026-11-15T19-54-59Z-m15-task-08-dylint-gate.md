# M15 Task 8: the dylint policy lint (warn) and the `dylint` gate

Issue #469, part of epic #460.

## Added
- `[workspace.metadata.dylint]` in `Cargo.toml`: the `hyp_tracing` library of hyp-rust-policy-lints, tag v0.1.2 (only that library; `hyp_hegel` is not loaded).
- `[lints.rust.unexpected_cfgs]` with `check-cfg = ["cfg(dylint_lib, values(any()))"]`, so `cfg_attr(dylint_lib = "hyp_tracing", allow(...))` exemptions do not warn in ordinary builds.
- An empty `[workspace]` table with `exclude = [".worktrees"]`.
- `just dylint`: `DYLINT_RUSTFLAGS="-W uninstrumented_fn" cargo dylint --all -- --all-targets`.
- `tools/agentic/gate.sh dylint`: runs `just dylint` under the shared gate lock with a 45 m limit (`AGENTIC_TIMEOUT_DYLINT`). Two shell tests in `tools/agentic/tests/run.sh`.
- `docs/contributing/policy-lints.md`: levels, `skip_all`, safe and validated fields, never `err` or `ret`, events and the `okf_mcp::local_only` target, MCP tools, exemptions and the accepted reasons (span makers `request_span`, `on_response` and `capture_request_span` included), how to run.

## Changed
- `tools/agentic/README.md`, `tools/agentic/brief-common.md` (Gates) and `AGENTS.md`'s table name the new gate.
- The M15 plan (Task 8, Task 10 and the Verified facts) records what was verified, in `> **Corrected (issue #469).**` notes.

## Notes
- dylint reads only `[workspace.metadata.dylint]`; `[package.metadata.dylint]` gives "No libraries were found". So the `[workspace]` table is needed.
- Without `exclude = [".worktrees"]`, a git worktree nested in the checkout whose `Cargo.toml` has no `[workspace]` fails with "current package believes it's in a workspace when it's not". With it, those branches keep building.
- The lint is deny by default. `-W uninstrumented_fn` makes it a warning and `gate.sh dylint` passes. Lint flags are not part of cargo's fingerprint, so after changing the level, touch `src/lib.rs`, `src/main.rs` and `src/bin/okf-maintenance.rs` (the plan's Task 10 says so).
- Warning count, which sizes the sweep: 1811 (1798 in the library, 1 in `okf-mcp`, 12 in `okf-maintenance`).
- `Cargo.lock`, `target/` and the `fmt`, `lint` and `test` gates are unaffected by the `[workspace]` table. The repository has no `.sqlx/` directory.
