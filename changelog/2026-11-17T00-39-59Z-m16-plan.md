# M16 design and plan: multi-replica serve

## Added

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-okf-multi-replica-design.md`: the design for running
  `okf-mcp serve` as several replicas (issue #551). An optional `[redis]` table keeps the
  state that today lives in one process:
  - MCP sessions and their key bindings;
  - OAuth pending authorizations and codes;
  - OIDC flows and passkey ceremonies.

  The design also covers two cross-instance announcements:
  - `admin_generation` bumps over pub/sub, so every instance reloads its key cache at once;
  - an OAuth access-token revocation counter.

  Without `[redis]` nothing changes.
- `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-okf-mcp-m16-multi-replica.md`: the plan for milestone
  16, in 12 tasks:
  - foundation: config and the Redis handle, the short-lived state abstraction, and the
    announcements;
  - the CLI announcement;
  - MCP sessions;
  - OAuth: access-token revocation and grant catch-up, codes, and pending authorizations;
  - OIDC;
  - passkeys;
  - the operations docs;
  - the close-out.

## Notes

- The plan's "Verified facts" come from the registry sources of rmcp 3.8.0, redis 1.8.2,
  testcontainers-modules 0.15.0, webauthn-rs 0.6.2 and rustls 0.23.46. A probe crate also

- Decisions taken on 2026-11-17 are recorded in the plan. The open questions at its end
