# M19 Task 06: the reader's boot, sign-in, account and sign-out

## Added
- `src/http/read/js/main.js`: the reader's boot and router. Boot registers
  the worker and waits (up to 5 s, on `controllerchange`) until it controls
  the page, so the first screen's reads are already cached; a failed or slow
  registration leaves the reader working online. A failed registration logs
  a `console.warn`, on a first visit and on an already-controlled page alike
  (review). It then reads
  `/api/v1/session`: a 401 deletes every offline copy and goes to
  `/signin?return_to=/read/#<route>`, and forgets the remembered catalog; a failed read (offline) renders from
  the caches. `show()` fixes each screen's catalog when it dispatches, so a
  refusal that answers after the user switched catalogs acts on the one it
  was about. A refusal runs its effects at once, whichever screen shows now
  (`effects()`: a 401 wipes and signs in, a lost catalog is evicted and
  forgotten, a missing concept is evicted), and draws only while its screen
  is still shown (`draw()`); `ctx.settle` and `ctx.drawWorst` let a screen
  with several reads run each refusal's effects as it arrives and draw the
  most serious one. The offline banner and the tab bar live here too.
- `src/http/read/js/refusals.js` (review): that refusal handling without the
  DOM. `show()` builds one handler per screen with the actions it may take
  (sign in, evict, remember, navigate, paint), so
  `tests/read_worker/refusals.test.mjs` runs it in Node with deferred reads:
  a late refusal after navigation, a 401 after navigation, `settle` acting
  before a sibling read settles, `drawWorst`'s severity, and handled
  refusals drawn but not acted on twice.
- `src/http/read/js/screens/me.js`: who is signed in and until when;
  Sign out deletes the offline copies and the remembered catalog first and then ends the session,
  keeping it (with a notice and a usable button) when the server cannot be
  reached; "Delete offline copies" works with no connection. Both go
  through `ctx.forget`, as a 401 does, so the next person to sign in on the
  device never sees the last one's catalog name (review). Sign-out is
  main.js's `ctx.signOut`: from its start (and a 401's) until the page
  leaves, a catalog opened meanwhile is not remembered, and the remembered
  catalog is deleted once more right before the redirect (second review).
- Placeholder screens `home.js`, `catalogs.js`, `tree.js`, `search.js`,
  `concept.js` and `document.js`, replaced in Tasks 7 to 9.
- Harness helpers `open_reader`, `sign_in_reader`, `wait_worker`,
  `go_offline` and `reload_reader` (`tests/ui_e2e/harness.rs`).
- `tests/ui_e2e/read.rs` with three journeys:
  `signing_in_returns_to_the_route_and_signing_out_wipes_the_caches` and
  `signing_out_without_a_connection_still_deletes_the_offline_copies`, and
  `a_catalog_opened_while_signing_out_is_not_remembered` (second review).

## Changed
- The plan's Task 6 is corrected under a dated note: the journey caches a
  copy before signing out (so it fails when sign-out stops wiping), checks
  the server ended the session, and gains the offline sign-out journey;
  `user_sessions` is imported by Task 11, its first user. A second dated
  note (review) covers `refusals.js`, `forget` and the registration
  warning, and the listings are the shipped files. The spec's sign-out text
  and its 401 row say the remembered catalog is forgotten too.
