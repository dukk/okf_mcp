## Release notes draft (milestone 18)

- **Removed metrics:** `okf_poll_duration_seconds`,
  `okf_catalog_refreshes_total`, `okf_catalog_concepts`,
  `okf_catalog_generation`; the `okf.poll` span. Replacements:
  `okf_cache_*` (by `cache`), `okf_invalidations_*`,
  `okf_housekeeping_*`.
- **Response shapes:** `GET /api/v1/catalogs`, `/whoami`, `/labels` and MCP
  `list_catalogs` return a page with `next`; `GET /api/v1/search` answers 400
  past `search_fanout_max` catalogs; `/healthz` reports `cached_catalogs`
  instead of `catalogs`; unbuildable catalogs are left out of listings.
- **New settings:** `catalog_cache_bytes` `"1GiB"`,
  `catalog_directory_entries` 100000, `catalog_ttl` `"10s"`,
  `credential_cache_entries` 100000, `user_cache_entries` 100000,
  `credential_ttl` `"10s"`, `overlay_cache_bytes` `"256MiB"`,
  `search_fanout_max` 25, `worker_shutdown_grace` `"30s"`.
- **Deprecated (parsed, warned, ignored):** `poll_interval`,
  `overlay_cache_size`, `mail.worker`.
- **Rolling deploy:** the M16 bridge keeps old and new replicas in step for
  one release.
